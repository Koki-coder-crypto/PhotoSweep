Keep the moments. Clear the rest.

Review your photos and videos, a month at a time. Clear choices, at your own pace.

Your month. One swipe at a time.

Choose a monthly thumbnail. Swipe right to keep, left to add a deletion candidate. Undo, skip and pick up where you left off.

Compare. Keep your favorite.

Review similar photos and duplicate images found on your iPhone. Dedicated lists help you browse large videos, screenshots and screen recordings.

A smaller copy. Still your moment.

Pro converts supported SDR videos to a maximum of 1080p or 720p. Preview and save the copy before deciding what to do with the original. HDR, slow motion and special edited formats are excluded.

One last look before deleting.

Review your candidates, then confirm with iOS. See the deleted item count and the size that could be verified. Deleted file size does not guarantee an equal increase in available device storage.

A little each day. More with Pro.

Free sorting includes 30 photos and 5 videos per day. Reviewing, deleting, undoing and rejudging use no extra allowance. Pro adds unlimited sorting, date filters, sorting order, larger batches and supported video compression. Prices and eligible trial terms appear before purchase.
