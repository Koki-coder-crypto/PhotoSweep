PHOTOSWEEP · IPHONE

A little help, right here.

Answers about photo access, deletion, compression and purchases.

Photos are missing

Check PhotoSweep's photo access in iPhone Settings. Limited access only shows the items you selected. Viewing or playing iCloud originals may require an internet connection.

Swiping does not delete

Left adds a deletion candidate. Right keeps the item. Open To delete, review your choices and confirm the iOS request before deletion.

Recover a deleted item

Items normally remain recoverable in the Photos app's Recently Deleted album for 30 days. iCloud Photos syncs deletion across devices on the same Apple Account. PhotoSweep cannot empty Recently Deleted.

Available storage has not changed

Recently Deleted, iCloud management and other apps can affect your device's measured free space. Deleted file size and available device storage are separate measurements.

Restore or cancel Pro

Use Restore purchases with the same Apple Account used to buy Pro. Manage or cancel a monthly subscription in iPhone Settings. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Pending purchases await Apple's approval process.

A video cannot be compressed

Compression supports standard SDR videos. Low storage, device temperature, connectivity and unsupported formats can prevent conversion. Keep the app open during conversion. Originals remain until saving and review are complete.
