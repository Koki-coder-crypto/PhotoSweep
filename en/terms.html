Before you start.

Conditions for PhotoSweep's features, purchases and media organization. Apple's standard end user licence agreement also applies.

Review your own choices

Similarity results do not guarantee that an item should be deleted. Check important items and review candidates before deleting. We do not guarantee storage savings or detection of every duplicate.

Free and Pro

Free new sorting includes 30 photos and 5 videos per day. Pro offers monthly and lifetime options. Check the App Store information displayed before purchase for actual prices, renewal periods and any eligible trial. Existing purchase rights are verified with StoreKit.

Renewal and purchase management

Monthly subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. Manage renewal and cancellation through Apple's subscription settings. Lifetime purchases do not renew. Refund requests can be made through Apple.

Video compression

Compression converts supported standard SDR videos. Quality may change, and some videos may not become smaller. Your original remains unless you add it to deletion candidates and confirm deletion. Keeping both copies does not imply an increase in free storage.
