PhotoSweep Support

Need help with PhotoSweep? Contact photosweep.support@gmail.com.

Photo access

Open iOS Settings → Apps → PhotoSweep → Photos to change photo-library access.

Restore Pro

Open PhotoSweep → Settings → Plan → Restore Purchases while signed into the Apple ID that made the purchase.

Deletion

PhotoSweep never deletes photos automatically. You choose photos and confirm deletion. iOS controls the final photo-library deletion behavior.
