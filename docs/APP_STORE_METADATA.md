# PhotoSweep — App Store metadata

## Name
PhotoSweep: Photo Cleaner

## Subtitle
Clean photos, privately

## Promotional text
Find easy cleanup wins in your camera roll. Review similar candidates, older photos, and high-resolution space hogs — privately on your iPhone.

## Description
PhotoSweep helps you take back space from a crowded camera roll without uploading your photo library.

START WITH THE EASY WINS
• Similar candidates: review photos taken moments apart with near-matching framing
• Space hogs: surface high-resolution photos likely to use more storage
• Older photos: quickly revisit photos more than a year old

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
PhotoSweep never auto-deletes photos. You select every item and confirm deletion.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Photo analysis happens on your device. Version 1.0 has no advertising SDK and requires no account.

PHOTOSWEEP PRO
Free users can remove up to 30 photos. PhotoSweep Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited cleanups and Pro review tools. The current local price is shown by the App Store before purchase.

Storage savings shown before deletion are estimates. Actual reclaimed storage can vary because of image compression, iCloud Photos optimization, and iOS storage behavior.

## Review notes
PhotoSweep requests Photo Library access to analyze lightweight metadata and display user-controlled cleanup candidates. No photo is deleted automatically. To test deletion: grant photo access, run a scan, open a review category, select photos, and tap Delete.

PhotoSweep Pro is the non-consumable product:
`com.kokicoder.photosweep.pro.lifetime`

A Restore Purchases action is available on the paywall. The app does not claim pixel-identical duplicate detection; “Similar candidates” is a review aid based on capture time, aspect ratio, and resolution.
